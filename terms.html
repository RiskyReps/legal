5. Fair play and integrity

Risky Reps only works if proofs are real. By playing, you agree:

  • No fake proofs. A proof must show you actually performing the set it claims — the right exercise, the full count, recorded for that challenge.
  • No cheating tools or impersonation. You may not submit someone else's performance as your own, edit a video to misrepresent a set, or otherwise deceive the game or another player.

The challenge system

  • The player who sent a challenge may challenge its proof while the proof still exists (within its 24-hour life), on one of two grounds: "Not the set" (the video is fake or off-topic) or "Short count" (real push-ups, but fewer than demanded).
  • The challenged player may Concede (accept the challenge, and the result converts immediately as if the set had lapsed) or Appeal (send the case to the referee). If the challenged player does nothing before the deadline, the case goes to the referee automatically.
  • The referee (the developer) reviews challenged and reported content and rules on each case. The referee's decision is final within the game.
  • Challenging costs the challenger 2 Points, which are paid to the performer if the challenge is rejected.

Consequences

If a challenge is upheld, the result is corrected to the lapse it should have been: the performer takes the lapse result, the sender receives the lapse outcome, and the proof is removed from Reels. Depending on the case and any prior strikes, we may also:

  • Issue a warning
  • Adjust Points (including fines)
  • Suspend or close the account

Integrity strikes are recorded on the account and are visible only to that player and the referee.
