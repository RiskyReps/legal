Risky Reps — Privacy Policy

Effective date: [Effective date is set when the beta opens] Contact: teamriskyreps@gmail.com

Risky Reps is a mobile game where friends challenge each other to push-up sets and record short video proofs. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and how long we keep it.

Who this applies to

Risky Reps is for users aged 13 and older. If you are under 13, please do not use the app. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we will delete it.

What we collect

Account information

When you sign in (by email, Google, or Apple), we receive and store:

  • Your email address and a unique account identifier from the sign-in provider
  • Your display name (the public name you choose in the app)
  • Your avatar choice
  • Your timezone (used so game timers and quiet times follow your local clock)

Game data

To run the game we store:

  • Challenges ("drops") you send and receive, their status, and their results
  • Your Points and Motion balances and the history of how they changed
  • Your game statistics (completions, streaks, head-to-head records, and similar)
  • Your friend connections and blocked players
  • Your notification settings, including your quiet times

Content you create

  • Video proofs — the short videos you record to prove a completed set
  • Photos used in the game's bounce feature
  • Reels — proofs you share to the in-app Reels feed
  • Reactions and other in-app interactions with that content

Push notification data

If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device so we can deliver game-event notifications (for example, that a challenge was sent to you). Pushes are held during your quiet times and delivered afterwards if they are still relevant. You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings or in your device settings.

Moderation data

If a proof is challenged or reported, or an upload is held by automated screening (see below), we store the case record: the content involved, the reason given, the players involved, and the outcome. This includes records of integrity strikes on an account. Strikes are private — only you can see your own.

Technical data

We keep the limited technical records needed to run and secure the service (such as server logs and error records). We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell personal data.

Video proofs: the 24-hour rule

Proofs are short-lived by design:

  • Unreported proofs are automatically deleted 24 hours after they are recorded, unless they are bookmarked by someone who can see them — a friend, or a player in the drop. Bookmarked media is kept while at least one bookmark holds it, and for 24 hours after the last bookmark is released.
  • If a proof is reported or challenged, it is kept only until that case is resolved, and is then deleted within 24 hours of the resolution.
  • Reels you share are removed on the same schedule as the proof they came from.
  • The performer in a video or photo can delete it everywhere at any time. If content is removed for a safety or policy reason, it is deleted immediately, together with every bookmark of it.

Bookmarks

Your bookmarks are private — only you can see what you have saved. A performer can see how many friends are keeping a piece of their media, never who. You can download only your own performances.

Who can see your content

  • A proof is visible to the players in that challenge.
  • A reel is visible to the players in the duel and to the performer's accepted friends, inside the app only.
  • Your display name, avatar, and game statistics are visible to other players in the app. Your Points balance is never shown to other players — only you can see it.

The referee and automated screening

Risky Reps has a referee (the developer) who keeps the game fair. Here is exactly what that means for your content:

  • The referee reviews only content that has been challenged or reported by a player, plus uploads held by automated screening. The referee does not browse or review other content.
  • Uploads are automatically screened for explicit nudity, sexual content, and gore. If an upload clearly hits one of those categories, it is held before posting and goes to the referee, who can release it or remove it.
  • In a referee case, the referee can see: the proof or photo, the set count that was demanded, the reason for the challenge or report, the players involved, and any prior integrity strikes on the account.
  • Referee decisions on integrity challenges can change a game's recorded result and adjust Points and Motion accordingly (see the Terms of Service).

Service providers

We use Supabase to provide our database, file storage (for proofs, photos, and reels), and sign-in authentication. Supabase processes this data on our behalf to run the app. Sign-in is also handled through your chosen provider (email, Google, or Apple). We do not sell your personal data to anyone, and we do not share it for advertising.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.

How long we keep things

  • Proofs and reels: deleted on the 24-hour schedule described above.
  • Account and game data: kept while your account exists.
  • Moderation records: kept with the account so the referee can see prior strikes when deciding a new case.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. Deleting your account erases your account's data, including:

  • Your sign-in credentials and profile (display name, avatar, timezone)
  • Your notification settings and quiet times
  • Your proofs, photos, and reels
  • Your friend connections and game records, including Points, Motion, and statistics

Your choices

  • You can change your display name and avatar in Settings.
  • You can control notifications and quiet times in Settings, and notifications in your device settings.
  • You can delete your account in Settings, as described above.
  • For anything else about your data, contact us at teamriskyreps@gmail.com.

Security

We use the security features of our providers, including encrypted connections and account-based access controls. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and limit access to what the game needs.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in an important way, we will update the effective date above and let users know in the app. Continuing to use Risky Reps after a change means you accept the updated policy.
